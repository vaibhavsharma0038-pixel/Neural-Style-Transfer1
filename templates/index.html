Neural Architecture

Real-time arbitrary artistic synthesis powered by deep neural representations.

01

VGG-19 Encoder

A deep 19-layer convolutional network extracts multi-scale spatial and textural activations up to the relu4_1 layer, separating pictorial content from surface style.

02

AdaIN Transformation

Adaptive Instance Normalization shifts the mean and variance of the content features to match the statistical profile of the style feature maps without altering structural topology.

t = σ(s) · (f_c − μ(f_c)) / σ(f_c) + μ(s)
03

Mirror Decoder

An inverted convolutional network trained with Reflection Padding and Nearest-Neighbor upsampling reconstructs the transformed feature statistics back into pristine RGB pixels.

04

Gram Correlation Loss

Multi-layer Gram matrix loss across relu1_1 through relu5_1 guides the decoder to faithfully emulate the brushstroke frequency and color harmony of master artists.
